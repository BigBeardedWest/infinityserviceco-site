Privacy Policy — Infinity FSM

Last updated November 19, 2026

Infinity FSM is the internal field-service management app used by Infinity Services Inc. ("Infinity Services", "we") to run its own work orders, scheduling, and billing. It is not offered to the public or to other companies. This policy explains what data the app handles, including data from QuickBooks Online.

Who uses the app

Only Infinity Services staff — technicians, dispatch, billing, and management. Each person signs in with their Apple ID, and access is limited by their role.

What data we handle

  • Work-order records: customers, service sites, equipment, labor and travel time, parts used, notes, photos, and signatures.
  • Staff accounts: name, email, role, and pay settings.
  • From our own QuickBooks Online company, once connected: customer names and terms, products and services, sales-tax codes, and invoices (number, date, lines, totals, balance, and status), including invoice PDFs.

How we use it

Only to operate Infinity Services: dispatching and completing service work, paying technicians, and creating and tracking invoices. The app reads from QuickBooks Online to match records and check totals, and it can create invoices in our QuickBooks company when billing staff choose to.

Where it is stored

On servers we operate at DigitalOcean in the United States (database and file storage). QuickBooks access tokens are stored encrypted. Data moves between the app and our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share this data with anyone outside Infinity Services, and we do not use it for advertising. Our service providers (DigitalOcean for hosting, Apple for sign-in and notifications, Intuit for QuickBooks Online, Microsoft for document storage) process data only to provide their services to us.

Retention and disconnecting

We keep business records for as long as we need them for operations, accounting, and legal requirements. QuickBooks Online can be disconnected at any time from the app's Settings or from the QuickBooks Apps page; once disconnected, the app stops reading from or writing to QuickBooks.
